PyroCryptic
by: Timothy Wall (BooBerry.Ck)

--Map Info-- 
1. Consists of Two sectors. Sector 1 has 4 objectives, 1 sabo, and 3 neut. Sector 2 has 5 objectives, 4 neut and 1 hdd extraction. In Sector 1, you must get 1 objective, and in the 2nd sector u must neutralize 2.
2.Blowing up the generators in sector 1 will turn off all modern lights in the level. 
3.For now have added minimum security consisting of only lasers and one motion detector. More will be added if need be. 
4.Sector 2 has a rails that if merc is pulled over, the time hes away will be just under 20 seconds. Its due to the drop to the lava below which is 7-8 seconds long. 
If that turns out to be too unbalancing, I'll change it. But i don't think it will be a problem. Rail pulls are one in a million. 
5.Haven't counted yet, but there are like at least 20-30 coops places. 
6. Sector two has traps (one side smashing walls, other side swinging axes) leading into the objectives. Each side has a button to activate or deactivate. But there is no way to tell if its on or off. 
7. There are no health boxes, but fountains of holy water for healing.
