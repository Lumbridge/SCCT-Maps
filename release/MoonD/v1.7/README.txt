Moonlight Park v1.7 - Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory Versus
Released 22 October 2026

An off-season British seaside amusement park at night. Package name:
MoonD.

CHANGES
- The security camera's alarm locks the CCTV recorders laptop while it
  sounds.
- Every laptop hack takes 20 seconds.
- The arcade fire escape is a gentler stair with a half landing, and its
  rails can be grabbed.
- The helter skelter now stands in the service yard by the spy station,
  with zip lines to the arcade roof and the park office roof (the ghost
  train zip line is gone).
- The hook-a-duck and water race stalls moved over to leave a walkway
  past the fire escape.
- New ledges: the hook-a-duck and water race name boards, the hook-a-
  duck roof sides, and inside the park office's south window.
- The disk drop-off by the dodgems is now in the spy station yard.
- No more lip to catch on at the top of the spy station stairs.
- Fixed shimmering on the fire escape.

INSTALL
Close the game, extract the ZIP and copy its Packages folder into your
Versus installation (the folder that contains Packages and System),
merging folders. Start Versus and select Moonlight Park.
All players need the same version.

Included playable files:
Packages/Maps/MoonD.sdc
Packages/Textures/MoonD-i.utc

Everything else the map uses comes with Enhanced SCCT Versus 4.0.

The editable map is in the repository at
release/MoonD/v1.7/Packages/MapsEd/MoonD.sdc, outside this ZIP.
