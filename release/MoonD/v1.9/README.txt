Moonlight Park v1.9
Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory Versus
18 November 2026

The two sloping catwalks now meet under the rollercoaster rails at the crest. The rails and their supports follow the turn continuously.

The Magic Mouse Store laptop (TermA) is available when the match starts with six or more players. The spies need four objectives to win at every lobby size.

The playable map uses a smaller package. Its meshes retain their render geometry and collision. The editable download includes the source geometry.

INSTALL

Close the game and extract this ZIP. Copy the Packages and System folders into your Enhanced SCCT Versus 4.0 installation, merging the folders. Select Moonlight Park in the map list. All players in a match need the same version.

PLAYER FILES

Packages/Maps/MoonD.sdc
Packages/Textures/MoonD-i.utc
System/_PC_/MoonD.int

The editable map is in the SCCT-Maps repository at release/MoonD/v1.9/Packages/MapsEd/MoonD.sdc.
