Moonlight Park v1.1 - Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory Versus
Released 13 October 2026

An off-season British seaside amusement park at night. Package name: MoonD.

CHANGES
- The haunted house and the breaker building are closer to the plaza.
- The ghost train and park office tunnel hatches now come up behind
  their buildings. The spy station has a second hatch.
- Many more ledge grabs, on props as well as buildings. Ledges meet at
  corners, so you can shimmy round them.
- Signs, fascias and the carousel roof are solid and can be climbed.
- Two laptops moved: the haunted house one and the wheel one.
- The dark tunnels are lit.
- Flap doors on the wheel motor room and the back of the punch stall.
- The Magic Mouse coaster is repainted.
- One ghost train figure is now a clown.

INSTALL
Close the game, extract the ZIP and copy its Packages folder into your
Versus installation (the folder that contains Packages and System),
merging folders. Start Versus and select Moonlight Park.
All players need the same version.

Included playable files:
Packages/Maps/MoonD.sdc
Packages/Textures/MoonD-i.utc

Everything else the map uses comes with Enhanced SCCT Versus 4.0.

The editable map is in the repository at
release/MoonD/v1.1/Packages/MapsEd/MoonD.sdc, outside this ZIP.
