Moonlight Park v1.10
Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory Versus

INSTALL
Close the game and extract the ZIP into your Enhanced SCCT Versus installation.
Merge the Packages and System folders. All players need the same version.
Select Moonlight Park in the map list. Open Packages/MapsEd/MoonD.sdc in
the Reloaded Editor to review the editable version.

MODES
Story: Complete any four park objectives. The Magic Mouse Store laptop
is available when six or more players are present at map load.
Disk Hunt: Spies collect disks. Mercs stop them.
Deathmatch: Spies and mercs face off.
Merc Deathmatch: Free-for-all with twelve dedicated merc spawns.

CHANGES
Rebuilt from the published v1.8 map with its full mesh source and collision
data. The sloping walks and rails meet at the rollercoaster crest.
Nine park props have updated shadow collision to fit the original engine's
per-mesh limit. Their visible meshes and player collision hulls are intact.
Includes the park's lobby and loading artwork, shorter team briefings,
and a map sound bank for victory, defeat and objectives.
