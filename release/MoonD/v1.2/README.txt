Moonlight Park v1.2 - Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory Versus
Released 14 October 2026

An off-season British seaside amusement park at night. Package name: MoonD.

CHANGES
- Callouts are more detailed: over a hundred named places, including
  roofs, rooms, hatches and tunnel sections.
- Ledge grabs on the arcade's change booth and claw grabbers.
- A low wall keeps mercs back from the spies' fence by the station.
- The coconut shy and shooting gallery roofs are solid.
- A new face-in-hole photo board on the midway.
- The zip line cables now sit on the lines you ride.
- More detail beyond the fence: the closed park, the car park, the
  town and the seafront street.
- Fixed shimmering on the west backdrop.

INSTALL
Close the game, extract the ZIP and copy its Packages folder into your
Versus installation (the folder that contains Packages and System),
merging folders. Start Versus and select Moonlight Park.
All players need the same version.

Included playable files:
Packages/Maps/MoonD.sdc
Packages/Textures/MoonD-i.utc

Everything else the map uses comes with Enhanced SCCT Versus 4.0.

The editable map is in the repository at
release/MoonD/v1.2/Packages/MapsEd/MoonD.sdc, outside this ZIP.
