Moonlight Park v1 - Splinter Cell: Chaos Theory Versus
Released 12 October 2026

An off-season British seaside amusement park at night. Package name: MoonD.

THE MAP
- Seven objectives, four to win: five laptops, a bomb site in the laser tag
  arena (three plant spots) and a disk to carry from the park office to a
  drop-off at the dodgems or the plaza kiosk.
- Rides that work: the Magic Mouse coaster with two cars you can ride, a
  turning carousel with carts to hide in, a Ferris wheel you can board, a
  ghost train, a walk-through haunted house and a helter skelter with two
  zip lines.
- A spy monorail from a protected spawn station to a station in the park.
- A tunnel network under the park with hatches, and gratings mercs can see
  and shoot through.
- Nine alarm cameras. Hacking the security terminal switches them all off
  for the round.
- Hacking the wheel's terminal sends the Ferris wheel out of control.
- A bomb in the laser tag arena blows holes in the building's walls.
- A main breaker that switches the park's lights and rides, for both sides.
- Park music, an animatronic band, an arcade, a cafe and parked dodgems.
- A train crosses the viaduct beyond the car park every few minutes.

INSTALL
Close the game and extract this ZIP. Copy the enclosed Packages folder into
your Versus installation directory, merging its folders. For standalone
Enhanced SCCT Versus, use the directory containing Packages and System.
Start Versus and select Moonlight Park.

Keep the filenames and subfolders intact. All players should use the same
map version.

Included playable files:
Packages/Maps/MoonD.sdc
Packages/Textures/MoonD-i.utc

Everything else the map uses comes with Enhanced SCCT Versus 4.0.

The matching editor source is available in the repository at
release/MoonD/v1/Packages/MapsEd/MoonD.sdc, outside this playable ZIP.
